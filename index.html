Recipe of the week · North Africa

One-pan shakshuka

Eggs poached in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce. Serves 2 in about 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 red pepper, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 can (400 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 4 eggs
  • Parsley, feta and bread to serve

Method

  1. Soften the onion and pepper in oil over medium heat, about 8 minutes.
  2. Add garlic and spices; cook for 1 minute.
  3. Pour in tomatoes, season, and simmer until thick, about 10 minutes.
  4. Make four wells, crack in the eggs, cover, and cook until the whites set.
  5. Top with parsley and feta. Serve with warm bread.
