How to build a mezze table that lasts all evening

Hummus, tabbouleh, labneh and the art of never letting the table look empty.

By Nour Haddad7 min read

A meal made of small plates

Mezze isn't a starter. In Lebanon it can be the whole evening: a parade of small dishes that arrive in waves while conversation flows.

The rule at my aunt's table is simple: if anyone can see the tablecloth, something is missing.

The essentials

Start with the dips: creamy hummus, smoky baba ghanoush and thick, tangy labneh drizzled with olive oil. Add tabbouleh, which in Lebanon is mostly parsley, with just a little bulgur.

Then come the warm plates: crispy falafel, fried halloumi, little meat pies called sfeeha and grilled vegetables brushed with garlic sauce.

Pacing the evening

Bring out cold dishes first, then hot ones as the night goes on. Keep warm flatbread coming, because it's both your plate and your spoon.

Finish with fresh fruit and small cups of coffee. Nobody should be in a hurry to leave.

A simple mezze for four

  • Hummus, labneh and baba ghanoush
  • Tabbouleh and a cucumber-tomato salad
  • Falafel or fried halloumi
  • Olives, pickles, fresh herbs and plenty of warm flatbread
